## Rules I write by

### Rule: No promised timelines

I don't commit to a delivery date I can't actually guarantee, especially before I've even started the fix.

- Wrong: "I will fix this in the fulture"
- Right: "Repro report on the way. Will follow up with a fix attempt."

### Rule: Name the version and the behavior

I name exactly what's broken and on what version, so the comment stands on its own without the reader re-opening the issue to remember what I mean.

- Wrong: "I can confirm this bug happens on my machine too."
- Right: "--style is ignored on v1.20.0, exactly as described."

### Rule: Say it like I'd say it out loud

No phrasing that reads like it was generated to sound engaged rather than to convey information.

- Wrong: "Very interesting project, love it!"
- Right: "Picking this up. I can reproduce it, repro report and fix attempt to follow."
